# 🎲 Satirical RNG Drops & Forge World Humor

## 💡 Concept
Throughout Eon’s world, the player occasionally finds **“meta-consumables”** — useless or mildly absurd items that parody modern gaming habits:  
gacha pulls, loot boxes, battle passes, and content gates.  

They exist purely for humor and world flavor, reinforcing the Forge’s philosophy: *you don’t need chance when you have craft.*

---

## 🧱 Example Drop Types

| Item Name | Flavor Text / Effect | Satirical Target |
|:------------|:----------------------|:------------------|
| **Premium Hope Token** | “Consume to imagine a better drop rate. Nothing happens, but you feel monetized.” | Gacha monetization |
| **Battle Pass Fragment** | “Collect all 100 to unlock mild disappointment.” | Seasonal passes |
| **Limited-Time Event Ticket** | “Expires the moment you pick it up.” | FOMO events |
| **Pre-Order Bonus Dust** | “It glows faintly, then apologizes.” | Pre-order exclusives |
| **Early Access Patch Note** | “Reads: ‘Balance coming soon™.’” | Unfinished releases |
| **Mystery Box (Deluxe Edition)** | “Right-click to realize gambling is a UI.” | Loot boxes |
| **Server Maintenance Token** | “Use to instantly disconnect from joy.” | Online-only design |
| **Subscription Tear** | “A single tear shed for monthly fees long gone.” | Paywalls |

Each is tagged as a `junk` type, with optional flavor effects like tiny particle bursts or temporary sound cues when used.

---

## 🗒️ Lore Notes & Quotes

Scattered around the world are **handwritten notes**, tablets, or console logs containing humorous one-liners in the tone of *Carlin-meets-Robin*:  

```
“Once there was a god of loot boxes. 
He randomized the world, then charged admission.”
— Fragment #12, The Gospel of Gacha
```

```
“Remember: the Forge gives you power. 
The Market just gives you probabilities.”
— Old Craftsman’s Diary
```

```
“They said true endgame was a season. 
I said true endgame was satisfaction.”
— Wanderer’s Graffiti, Map Tier 110
```

You can spawn these as world flavor items, readable via a lightweight text UI.

---

## 🎯 Design Purpose
- Inject humor without breaking immersion.  
- Reinforce Eon’s anti-RNG, pro-crafting identity.  
- Give explorers rewarding little *meta Easter eggs*.  
- Offer levity between intense Forge or Pinnacle runs.

> “Not every drop must change your build—some just change your mood.”
